Motivation

EcoRouter

Routing requests across GenAI platforms involves more considerations

Existing model routers optimize for cost or latency alone — none jointly enforce energy, privacy, and output quality at request time.

✕
Cost-only routers optimize dollars or latency, blind to on-device energy and battery impact.
✕
Cloud-first routers assume every prompt is safe to ship off-device — no PII-aware exclusion.
✕
Quality-agnostic routers pick the cheapest destination without checking it's good enough for this prompt.
✓
Physical cost modeled and scored per device, live, on every request.
✓
Privacy regex PII gate excludes cloud for sensitive prompts.
✓
Quality calibrated per-prompt estimator checks sufficiency before routing.

No always-one-device policy is safe by default: phone is fast and cheap but wrong on more than half of math/logic prompts; PC and cloud are accurate but ~9.5x and ~560x phone's energy respectively; cloud also leaks every PII prompt it's fed. EcoRouter's balanced profile matches PC/cloud-level quality while cutting energy 72% vs. always-cloud and keeping zero privacy violations.
